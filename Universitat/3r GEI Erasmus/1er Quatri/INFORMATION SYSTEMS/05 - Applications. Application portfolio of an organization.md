### CRM: Customer relationship management

**Focus:** clients as a key of success
	Personalized relationship with them:
		1. Satisfaction
		2. Value of the client for our organization

#### Which organizations can benefit from CRM?

It depends on several factors such as:
- Frequency
- Duration
- Number of clients

CRM is important when we have a MULTI-CHANNEL interaction.
CRM is more a strategical decision rather that a just a technical solution.

#### Modules goals:

1. Marketing: 
	1. Try to increase the fidelity of our clients.
	2. Provide activities for supporting proselling and upselling activities.
2. Sales automation:
	1. Support all the activities in the selling process and automatize them.
3. Support service:
	1. All that happens after the sell. Solve the problems that can occur once the sell is done.
4. Reporting:
	1. Generate reports about the data to support the operational CRM activities.
5. Analysis:
	1. Analise different clusters of clients that will be used in the marketing campains.
6.  Prediction:
	1. Understand the possible behaviour of the clients.


### How do we decide to apply ERM and CRM decisions.

ERP and CRM are mainly aquired in a buy modality.

For every COTS (commercial off the shelf) we need to choose the best provider for our organization. 

#### COTS choice process:
1. Requirements elicitation (functional/ non functional requirements).
2. Solution search: search the possible options.
3. Solution evaluation: compare the solutions available in the market to understand which is the best for our organization.
4. Evaluate the vendors: if the vendor is not reliable altough the solution is good we might not take it. Also we need to know how and where they operate among other things. 
5. Meeting with vendors: to discuss the implementation.
6. Request for quotation: to understand how much the solution cost
7. Selection: we choose the best solution after all the process.

**Solution evaluation:**
- We need to analize different perpectives
	- Functional perspective:
		- Coverage degree.
		- Personalization degree.
	- Architectural perspective:
		- Scalability.
		- Interoperability.
		- Security.
	- Cost
		- We need to consider all the costs and subcosts of the solution.
