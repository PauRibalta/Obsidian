## Information systems

Set of **resources**, **knowledge** and **technical skills** to manage the **information**.

### Goal of an Information System
- Data management.
- Defining and supporting process for activities and procedures.
- Automation and IT (technical infrastructure).

In an organization, an It is related with many other IT and provides outputs to other organizations, both external organs of the organization.


## Enterprise architectures (EA)

A map of the information system.
Desribe the IS in its current state (1) (AS IT IS) and support its evolution (2) (TO BE).
- Alignment inter-functional.
- Alignment between organisational layers.
- Alignment inter-organisational.

1: Abstract description of the components and their relationships.
	Components:
		- Data, applications, resources, processes, technologies.

2: Prescribes the IS evolution overtime.

ZACHMAN framework:

It provides a taxonomy to organize an EA and its components, organizing everything in a matrix.
